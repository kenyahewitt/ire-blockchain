Unique agents

Every id 1–5000 has its own traits, strategy, venue, timeframe, max notional, and a primary asset no other token holds. Secondary is a hedge pair. Min $1M mcap is a mandate filter, not a claimed floor or market cap.

Paper vs live

Paper records simulated marks from a public quote if one is fetchable. If the quote fails, the blotter goes offline — it will not invent prices or PnL. Live is an owner-signed intent (personal_sign) only. No swap-router calldata. No eth_sendTransaction for trades. No custody.
