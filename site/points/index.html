Points

Use the chain, earn points

Points score activity on ire-1 in the last ~80 blocks: sends, inscriptions, markets, validator signups, and funding the Volt. They are a usage score, not IRE, not a listed token, and not a promise of money. Airdrops from the vault can pay IRE to point holders later, by governance.

Send or receive

Inscribe

5 points if the memo starts with IREINSCRIBE1 (profile, token, art, file).

Markets

10 points for an ire-bet open, buy, resolve, or analyze.

Fund the Volt

20 points for fund-community-pool (paying into the vault that later pays rewards and airdrops).

Validator signup

25 points if the memo contains IREVAL1 (Crypto Brokers wallet signed up as an ire-1 validator candidate). Waitlist, not a consensus seat. Testnet. Not money.

What you can get
