# IRE usage points

Points score **using `ire-1`**. They are not `uire`, not a bank denom, and not a promise of money.

`ire-1` is a public testnet. Do not put real value on it.

Live board: `/points/` (last ~80 blocks, GET `/rpc/` only).

## What you get

| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| Confirmed send/receive (tx touches your `ire1`) | 1 |
| Memo starts with `IREINSCRIBE1` | +5 |
| `ire-bet` market memo | +10 |
| `fund-community-pool` (pay into the Volt) | +20 |

Today that score is public. Later, governance can spend from the [IRE Vault](VAULT.md) to airdrop IRE, pay usage rewards, or cover fees. Points do not mint IRE. Inflation stays 0.

## What this is not

Not a listed token. Not tokenfactory. Not a brokerage. Empty window = nobody used the chain, not a fake leaderboard.
